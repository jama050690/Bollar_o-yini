Qisqacha

"Aqlli Do'stlar" hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumot yig'maydi, hech kimga yubormaydi va internetdan foydalanmaydi. Barcha ma'lumotlar faqat sizning qurilmangizda saqlanadi.

1. Ilova haqida

"Aqlli Do'stlar" — 5–12 yoshli bolalar uchun ta'limiy o'yin. Ilova to'liq oflayn ishlaydi va internetga ulanish ruxsatini so'ramaydi.

2. Qurilmada saqlanadigan ma'lumotlar

O'yin ishlashi uchun quyidagilar faqat qurilmaning o'zida saqlanadi:

  • Bola profili: ism (yoki laqab), yosh va tanlangan avatar;
  • O'yin natijalari: yulduzlar, o'ynalganlar soni, o'yin vaqti;
  • Kunlik ekran vaqti hisobi;
  • Ota-ona sozlamalari: ota-ona paneli PIN kodi va kunlik vaqt chegarasi.

Bu ma'lumotlar biz yoki boshqa birov tomonidan ko'rilmaydi, serverga yuborilmaydi va uchinchi tomonlarga berilmaydi.

3. Biz nimalarni qilmaymiz

  • Reklama ko'rsatmaymiz;
  • Ilova ichida xarid yo'q;
  • Analitika, kuzatuv (tracking) yoki reklama identifikatorlaridan foydalanmaymiz;
  • Joylashuv, kamera, mikrofon, kontaktlar yoki fayllarga kirish so'ramaymiz;
  • Ijtimoiy tarmoqlar yoki tashqi havolalar yo'q.

4. Ovozli o'qish

Ba'zi o'yinlarda so'z va raqamlarni ovoz chiqarib aytish uchun qurilmaga o'rnatilgan nutq sintezi (Text-to-Speech) xizmatidan foydalaniladi. Ilova bu xizmatga faqat aytiladigan so'zni beradi. Ilovaning o'zi hech qanday ma'lumotni internetga yubormaydi.

5. Bolalar maxfiyligi

Ilova bolalar uchun mo'ljallangan va bolalardan shaxsiy ma'lumot yig'maydi. Bu COPPA, GDPR-K va Google Play'ning "Families" siyosati tamoyillariga mos keladi. Ota-ona paneli PIN kod bilan himoyalangan.

6. Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Barcha ma'lumotlarni o'chirish uchun ilovani qurilmadan o'chiring yoki Sozlamalar → Ilovalar → Aqlli Do'stlar → Xotira → Ma'lumotlarni tozalash ni bosing. Bizda saqlanadigan nusxa yo'q.

Summary

"Aqlli Do'stlar" does not collect, share or transmit any personal data and does not use the internet. All data stays on your device.

1. About the app

"Aqlli Do'stlar" ("Smart Friends") is an educational game for children aged 5–12. The app works fully offline and does not request internet access.

2. Data stored on the device

To make the game work, the following is stored only on the device:

  • Child profile: name (or nickname), age and chosen avatar;
  • Game results: stars, number of plays, play time;
  • Daily screen-time counter;
  • Parental settings: parent panel PIN and daily time limit.

This data is never seen by us or anyone else, is never sent to a server and is never shared with third parties.

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising;
  • No in-app purchases;
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers;
  • No access to location, camera, microphone, contacts or files;
  • No social features or external links.

4. Text-to-speech

Some games read words and numbers aloud using the text-to-speech engine built into the device. The app only passes the word to be spoken to that engine. The app itself sends no data over the internet.

5. Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and collects no personal information from them, in line with COPPA, GDPR-K and the Google Play Families Policy. The parent panel is protected by a PIN.

6. Deleting data

To delete all data, uninstall the app or open Settings → Apps → Aqlli Do'stlar → Storage → Clear data. We keep no copy.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
