**Full description** (up to 4000 characters):
```
🧠 Aqlli Do'stlar ("Smart Friends") is a collection of learning games for children aged 5 to 12.

Learning content is in Uzbek. In the Alphabets and Translator games, children also explore Russian and English letters and words. The app interface is available in Uzbek, Russian and English (chosen in the parent panel).

Your child enters their age and the app opens the games that fit it. Every game has 3 difficulty levels, and results are rated with stars.

🐣 LITTLE ONES (ages 5–7)
• Memory — find matching cards
• Letters & Numbers — learn letters and digits
• Colors & Shapes — sort colors and shapes
• Counting, Alphabets, Times tables
• Build with shapes, Coloring
• Find the pair, Patterns

🚀 MIDDLE GROUP (ages 8–9)
• Math adventure, Arithmetic, Fractions
• Word builder, Mini crossword, Translator
• Maze quiz, Mini Sudoku
• Clock — tell the time
• Shop — count money and change

🏆 BIG KIDS (ages 10–12)
• Equations, Sequences, Percentages, Speed math
• Geometry — perimeter and area
• Geography — countries and capitals
• Nature — animals, plants, Earth and sky
• Anagram, Translator+, Sudoku
• 🤖 Robot coder — first steps in coding with blocks: sequences and loops

👨‍👩‍👧 FOR PARENTS
• Separate profiles for several children
• Parent panel protected by a PIN
• Daily play time limit (15 / 30 / 45 / 60 minutes)
• A report for each child: play time, stars, favorite games

🛡️ SAFE AND CALM
• No ads
• No in-app purchases
• No internet needed — works fully offline
• No data is collected; everything stays on the device
• Mistakes are never punished — just "Try again! 💪"
```
